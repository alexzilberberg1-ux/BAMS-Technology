  1. Ask “Do you take Delta Dental PPO?” — it answers only from the practice's real plan list.
  2. Say “I need a cleaning this Saturday” — it checks live availability and offers real slots.
  3. Pick a time and give a name and phone number — it books the appointment in the scheduling system.
  4. Ask for a human — it records a callback request for the front desk.
  5. Try asking for medical advice — it politely refuses and routes you to the providers.
